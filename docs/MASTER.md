Phase 4: The agent pipeline (Monitor to Measure)
Follow /docs/MASTER.md. Phase 4 only. Work in /apps/api/agents. This is the heart of the project: build real logic, not templates around an LLM.GOALAn orchestrator that, on every few ticks or on any event, runs seven agents in order, shares one typed Case object, and produces a ranked list of recommended actions, each with evidence, a plan, an impact estimate and an approval requirement.SHARED OBJECTS (pydantic)Evidence {id, kind, source, block_or_node, tick_range, value, baseline, note}Case {id, type (leak, shortage, demand_spike, sensor_fault, quality), location, confidence, evidence[], status}Plan {steps[ordered: tool, args, reason], expected_impact, risk, needs_approval, vulnerable_flag, rollback}Trace {agent, started, ended, ms, inputs[], outputs[], notes}AGENTS1 Monitor: reads latest ticks, checks data quality (stale, flat-line, out of range, dropout). Flags suspect sensors so later agents do not mistake them for leaks.2 Detector: per-block anomaly detection. Use minimum night flow versus the 90-day baseline (z-score) plus an IsolationForest on [flow, pressure, hour, weekday, occupancy flag]. A leak case needs agreement from at least two signals (for example night flow up and downstream pressure down, or tank level falling faster than metered use). One signal alone becomes a sensor_fault or watch case.3 Localizer: zone mass balance (inlet and tank outflow versus the sum of block meters) and the pressure gradient along the pipe graph to pick the most likely pipe segment. Return top 3 segments with probabilities and the evidence behind each.4 Forecaster: demand forecast per block for 7 days (Holt-Winters with weekday seasonality and an occupancy regressor), and a 72-hour tank and sump level forecast using forecast demand, borewell yield, rainfall and weather forecast. Output the days of reserve left with an 80 percent band. Always compare against a naive baseline and record the error.5 Prioritizer: score each open case with a transparent formula and show the terms: loss rate in kL per day, people affected, vulnerable weight, time to critical level, repair cost and crew availability. Output a ranked repair queue and the score breakdown. Never a black box.6 Optimizer: a linear program (scipy linprog) that decides pump schedule, tank fill order and valve routing to minimise energy and shortage hours, subject to constraints: every vulnerable block keeps at least its minimum supply, no tank below its safety level, pump run-time limits. Return the plan and the before/after comparison. Also generate conservation actions, such as shifting hostel pump timing, mess cleaning-water hours, STP recycled water for gardens and flushing, and quote the expected saving of each.7 Interventor and Measurer: converts the plan into tool calls (below), puts anything consequential in the approval queue, and after approval runs a counterfactual simulation to estimate impact: litres saved, rupees saved, hours of reserve gained, repair time avoided. After execution it compares the real result with the estimate and stores the error.VERIFIER (runs after agents 2 to 6)For each claim in a Case or Plan: check that it cites Evidence IDs, that the evidence exists, that the numbers match, and that at least two independent sources support a leak or shortage claim. If not, downgrade confidence or reject and log why. Output a groundedness score per case.TOOLS (simulated enterprise systems, in /apps/api/tools)create_cmms_ticket(block, segment, priority, summary), set_valve(valve_id, state), set_pump_schedule(pump_id, schedule), draft_advisory(audience, text), notify(role, message). Valve and pump changes and advisories that touch vulnerable blocks are always needs_approval. Each tool call writes to the audit log and can be rolled back.REASONING TEXTFor each Case, produce a 'Why' block: 3 to 5 sentences made only from Evidence values, ending with evidence IDs in brackets. Use the LLM wrapper to phrase it, then run the Verifier; if the LLM is down or fails verification, use the deterministic template.EXPOSEGET /api/cases, GET /api/cases/{id} (evidence, why, plan, trace), GET /api/queue (ranked repairs), GET /api/forecast, GET /api/plan, POST /api/approvals/{id} {decision, note} (admin only), GET /api/audit, GET /api/traces.ACCEPTANCE- Inject a hostel night leak: within 3 simulated hours the Detector opens a leak case, the Localizer ranks the true segment in its top 3, and the Why text cites at least 3 evidence IDs.- Inject a meter fault: no leak case is opened; a sensor_fault case is.- Two simultaneous faults get ranked with a visible score breakdown, and changing a weight in the Prioritizer settings reorders them.- Rejecting a plan removes it from the queue and writes an audit row.- Each agent's latency is recorded in its Trace.- Unit tests for Detector, Prioritizer, Optimizer constraints (vulnerable minimum always holds).
